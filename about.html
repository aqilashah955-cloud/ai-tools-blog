About

Every "free" AI tool hides a catch — credits that run out in a day, a watermark stamped on your export, a free tier you are not legally allowed to publish with. This blog exists so you find the catch before you waste hours on it.

I run AutoTube Lab, a YouTube channel where I test free AI tools on their actual free tiers — video generators, voice generators, and the tools behind faceless video creation — and rank them on the only three things that matter: what you actually get for free, whether you can download it, and whether you are allowed to publish it.

Read a test here before you sign up anywhere, and you will know in five minutes what most people learn after a week of dead ends: which tool is genuinely free, which one is a trial in disguise, and which one is worth your time at all.

Why trust these tests

  • Tested, not summarized. Every tool is tried on its real free tier — the credits, the export, the watermark, the licence — not quoted from its marketing page.
  • Same checks, every time. Free allowance, download, watermark, resolution, and commercial use, so tools can be compared fairly.
  • Figures checked. Prices and limits are verified against the tools' official pricing pages, and the date of the check is on the post.
  • No hype. No invented statistics, no testimonials, no promises about earnings. If something was tested, the post says how. If it wasn't, the post says so.
