About

Hi, I'm Aqila Shah. I have a Master's degree in English Literature and I have worked as a senior English teacher for 8 years.

I also run AutoTube Lab, a YouTube channel where I test free AI tools — video generators, voice generators, and the tools behind faceless video creation — and report honestly on what the free tiers actually give you.

This blog is the written version of that work, plus the practical side of it: how to use the tools, how to automate small jobs, how to write with AI without losing your voice, and how to set up simple routines that save time.

What this blog is

  • For beginners. Every tutorial assumes no prior knowledge and explains each step.
  • Practical. Steps you can follow today, not theory.
  • Honest about "free". Free tiers have catches — credit limits, watermarks, licences. The tutorials say what those catches are, and the figures are checked against the tools' official pricing pages.
  • No hype. No invented statistics, no testimonials, no promises about earnings. If something was tested, the post says how. If it wasn't, the post says so.

What this blog is not

It is not a news site and not a review mill. New tutorials appear when a tool has actually been tested or a workflow has actually been used — not on a schedule.
